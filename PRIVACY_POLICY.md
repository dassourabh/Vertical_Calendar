# Privacy Policy — Vertical Calendar

**Effective Date:** 24 October 2026

Vertical Calendar (“the App”) is an Android calendar application developed by Sourabh Das. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Overview

Vertical Calendar provides calendar navigation, regional calendar support, event management, countdown events, Google Calendar synchronization, and a home screen widget.

The App:
- Stores event information locally on your device.
- Can synchronize events with Google Calendar when you sign in with a Google account and grant the required calendar access permission.
- Does not operate a private server to store user calendar data.

Google and Android may independently process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 2. Information We Handle

### 2.1 Calendar and Event Information

The App allows you to create and manage calendar events and countdown events.

Event information may include:
- Event titles and descriptions.
- Event dates and times.
- Other event details that you enter or choose to synchronize.

This information may be stored locally on your device to provide the App's features.

### 2.2 Google Calendar Synchronization

The App supports synchronization with Google Calendar.

To use this feature, you must sign in with a Google account and grant the required calendar access permission.

When synchronization is enabled, the App accesses and exchanges relevant calendar information with Google Calendar to provide the synchronization functionality.

The App developer does not operate a private server to store this information. Information synchronized with Google Calendar is handled by Google in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

You can review or revoke the App's access through your Google Account settings. Revoking access may prevent calendar synchronization from functioning.

### 2.3 Regional Calendars

The App currently supports Bengali and Telugu regional calendars.

These features provide regional date information. Any additional information processed depends on the App's actual implementation.

### 2.4 Home Screen Widget

The App provides a home screen widget that displays the current date and day.

The widget is intended to provide convenient access to calendar information without requiring you to open the App.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App is used to:
- Display calendar dates and regional calendar information.
- Save and display events and countdowns.
- Synchronize events with Google Calendar when you authorize this feature.
- Display the current date and day through the home screen widget.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Calendar and countdown event information may be stored locally on your device.

When you enable Google Calendar synchronization, relevant information is exchanged with Google Calendar using the permissions you grant.

The App does not operate its own private server for storing user calendar information.

The App developer does not intentionally sell users' personal information. This policy does not cover independent data processing by Google, Android, or other services you choose to use.

If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, crash-reporting tools, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated as appropriate to reflect those data practices.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android's behavior and any device backups.

Events synchronized with Google Calendar may remain in your Google account until you delete them from Google Calendar.

Uninstalling the App or revoking its calendar permission does not necessarily delete events that have already been synchronized with Google Calendar. You can manage or delete those events directly in Google Calendar.

## 6. Data Security

The App relies on Android's security features and the security measures provided by the services used for synchronization.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their devices and Google Accounts secure and grant permissions only when they intend to use the corresponding features.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose calendar utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If you have concerns about information handled by the App, please contact the developer using the contact details provided below.
